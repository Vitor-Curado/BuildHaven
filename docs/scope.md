# Scope

## A place for my resume that's not LinkedIn

I want it to be a place recruiters and hiring managers can

a) see my resume
b) be impressed
c) download my resume

and also the whole login is simply because I want to be able to login from anywhere in the world and modify my resume. It's also a healthy goal for me to implement login, auth, CSR, CMS, and everything in between.

## Spin the wheel and see where it lands

Learning how to install PgBouncer? Trying CSR with TS and React for the first time? Do we go with a traditional ORM, or do we go as raw SQL as we can? What do we maintain, what do we outsource? That is what this is for. I have a GitHub project, currently it's sitting in DigitalOcean, and with it I can touch Linux-related stuff (from prod's CLI, OFC), cloud stuff (I have been meaning to migrate to an AWS EC2 instance for a while), or... you know... regular programming stuff.

It's my Phineas and Ferb's backyard, I can do whatever the hell I want and that is the appeal. It's where I can be allowed to see a shiny object or a butterly and follow it. A place to be allowed to make mistakes with zero expectations and no stakes. Nobody cares if my website is down. I mean, I do, but nobody else does, and that's a plus (+), not a minus (-). A place to `set expectations`, to learn the weight that is each and every decision.

## Potentially, a product

As I get the ropes of what goes where, how to twist, bend, deploy it and so fort, I can start involving other people. Perhaps a local business that currently only is in a Facebook group, perhaps a friend needs a website. It could even become a commercial product. 

I wouldn't, though, before I feel confident in: 

* `Client Sider Rendering`, basically `TypeScript` and `React`
* `Static Site Generation`
* Being able to mix SSR, CSR and SSG (I still have issues learning how to share the static asset pipeline)
* `Payments` (like Peter Thiel's Stripe payments API)
* `Migrations` (those are hard, okay? Mentally and emotionally)

Then, I would feel comfortable offering some of my projects as commercial products/services, but even then honestly the idea doesn't even come to me. Right now it's just my virtual backyard. Besides, I already promised four friends I'm making them a website when I can't even control mine well enough.
